\section*{S1. Experimental identity and computing environment}

The final study matrix contained 90 completed Swin-S fine-tuning runs: 10
paired flat and dual runs per dataset and five matched runs per dataset for each
of the MAE and $\mathrm{KL}(q\|p^h)$ consistency variants. The primary seeds
were 2026--2035; the penalty seeds were 2026--2030. Every retained run completed
30 epochs and used validation macro-F1 for checkpoint selection.

\section*{S2. Dataset construction and integrity details}

\paragraph{NPD-Rice-COCO-43.}
The 93,388 paths represented 93,362 unique SHA-256 hashes and 51,758 source
families. The local composite comprised 87,425 paths from the New Plant
Diseases collection, 3,355 Rice Leafs images, and 2,608 selected COCO images
used as a supervised background class. Twenty-six duplicate-hash clusters were
retained, but every exact match and every identified augmentation family was
assigned wholly to one split. The final split contained 74,700 training, 9,355
validation, and 9,333 test paths; the test set contained 5,171 source families.
Every class occurred in every split. Assignment-group, source-family, and exact
SHA-256 intersections were zero for every pair of partitions.

\paragraph{MultiCrop-88.}
The input inventory contained 79,086 paths. We removed 1,254 redundant paths
from 1,232 same-label exact-hash clusters and excluded all 62 paths from 29
cross-label conflict clusters. The 77,770 retained hashes were unique. The
resulting split contained 62,214 training, 7,787 validation, and 7,769 test
images, with all 88 classes represented in every partition. The local class
vocabulary strongly matches the published 91-class composite of Ahamed et al.
after exclusion of three mulberry classes, but the original assembly manifest
and licenses were not supplied. The collection is therefore described as a
local derivative. Its manifest \texttt{family\_id} is an exact-hash assignment
unit, not a separately verified acquisition family.

\paragraph{Agri-Foundation-145k.}
The public Zenodo record reports 144,751 images in 215 classes. The locked local
derivative contained 144,752 paths: a development pool later divided into
92,571 training and 23,140 validation images, plus a separate 29,041-image test
set. The one-path difference is a synthetic $10\times10$ file named
\texttt{dummy.jpg} in class
\texttt{cabbage\_cabbage\_black\_rot\_bing\_-\_copy}. It was the only
development support for that joint class. The file is not an observed plant
image and must not be interpreted as biological evidence. The exact-hash audit
found no development-to-test match. All 215 classes occurred in training and test;
212 occurred in validation.

The locked results retain the placeholder-supported class so that every
configuration is evaluated on the experiment that was actually run. Because all
models use the same derivative, seed-paired architecture comparisons remain
case matched. Nevertheless, the class's absolute F1 and its contribution to
215-class macro-F1 are not representative of ordinary learned performance. The
source archive contains a script named \texttt{ds2\_placeholder\_sensitivity.py}
that recomputes accuracy and macro-F1 after excluding this class. The numerical
outputs of that script were not present in the supplied archive and are
therefore not reported or inferred here. They should be generated, checked, and
deposited before submission.

Exact byte hashing cannot detect relationships created only by resizing,
recompression, cropping, color adjustment, or other transformations. No
perceptual-hash or embedding-similarity overlap audit was available for
MultiCrop-88 or Agri-Foundation-145k. The supported claim is therefore exact
identity control, plus source-family control only for NPD-Rice-COCO-43.

\section*{S3. Pairing, equivalence decisions, and event-scale context}

Flat and no-penalty dual models used identical seed sets (2026--2035) within
each dataset. The primary equivalence margin was $\pm0.25$ percentage points.
A dataset and metric result was considered practically equivalent only when its
complete 90\% TOST interval lay inside the margin and the Holm-adjusted decision
passed at $\alpha=0.05$ across the nine primary decisions. Five cells met both
requirements: accuracy, macro-F1, and CHER on NPD-Rice-COCO-43, plus accuracy
and CHER on MultiCrop-88.

The complete nine-decision Holm correction was repeated post hoc at
$\pm0.10$ and $\pm0.50$ points. At the stricter margin,
NPD-Rice-COCO-43 accuracy and CHER and MultiCrop-88 CHER remained
equivalent. At $\pm0.50$ points, Agri-Foundation-145k accuracy and CHER also
met equivalence, while macro-F1 remained unresolved on MultiCrop-88 and
Agri-Foundation-145k. These analyses show which conclusions are robust to the
choice of negligible-effect threshold.

Mean flat/dual cross-host event counts were 3.5/2.2 of 9,333 test images,
26.1/23.8 of 7,769 images, and 964.5/911.4 of 29,041 images for the three
datasets. A 0.10-point CHER margin corresponds to 9.3, 7.8, and 29.0 events; a
0.25-point margin corresponds to 23.3, 19.4, and 72.6 events; and a 0.50-point
margin corresponds to 46.7, 38.8, and 145.2 events. All intervals characterize
between-seed variation on fixed test sets, not sampling variation over new
populations.

\section*{S4. Family-balanced NPD-Rice-COCO-43 sensitivity analysis}

Each NPD-Rice-COCO-43 test image was assigned weight equal to the inverse of
its source-family size, giving every one of the 5,171 test families total weight
one. Flat/dual family-balanced means were 99.033/99.020\% accuracy,
98.522/98.491\% macro-F1, and 0.0216/0.0107\% CHER. Paired dual-minus-flat
effects were $-0.0129$ points (95\% CI $[-0.0829,0.0571]$), $-0.0307$ points
($[-0.1352,0.0739]$), and $-0.0110$ points ($[-0.0241,0.0022]$), respectively.
The near-null result therefore persisted when each original family, rather than
each augmented path, had equal total influence.

\section*{S5. Consistency-penalty implementation and validation}

The secondary block contained five matched seeds for the no-penalty dual model,
MAE, and $\mathrm{KL}(q\|p^h)$ on each dataset. The dual objective used
condition cross-entropy plus host cross-entropy with $\alpha=1$. Both
consistency terms used $\lambda=1$. MAE was averaged over samples and host
categories. KL was implemented as $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q\|p^h)$, summed over host
categories and averaged over samples, with
$\epsilon=\texttt{torch.finfo}(p^h.\texttt{dtype}).\texttt{eps}$ in both
logarithms. The condition-implied host distribution $q$ was not detached, so
the consistency gradient passed through both the condition and host branches.

The available validators checked run completion, 30-row training histories,
selected checkpoints, split identities, and saved test predictions. Stored best
validation macro-F1 values were checked against the corresponding histories,
and accuracy, macro-F1, and CHER were reconstructed from saved labels and
standard predictions where available. The fixed unit coefficients were not
selected from locked-test performance.

\section*{S6. Complete head-disagreement and TCI results}

HDR compares the host implied by the condition argmax with the direct host-head
argmax. TCI was implemented by masking condition classes to the host predicted
by the direct host head. The operation reduced post-TCI HDR to zero in every
one of the 60 dual-head runs by construction. Table~\ref{tab:s-tci-hdr-full}
reports the complete configuration-level means. Flat runs are absent because
they have no independent host prediction.

For the 10 no-penalty Agri-Foundation-145k dual runs, TCI changed a mean of
332.3 predictions per seed. Among those disagreements, the host head alone
matched the labeled host in 38.7\%, the condition-implied host alone matched it
in 26.8\%, and both were wrong in 34.5\%. TCI gained 79.1 and lost 74.4 correct
top-1 decisions per seed, leaving a mean net gain of 4.7 cases.

\section*{S7. Long-tail, host-level, oracle, and calibration evidence}

The 215 Agri-Foundation-145k classes were grouped by training support into
1--25, 26--100, 101--500, and more than 500 images. Mean dual-minus-flat
within-stratum macro-F1 changes were 0.871, 0.600, 0.854, and 0.035 points,
respectively. Only the 101--500 stratum had a paired 95\% interval wholly above
zero. These comparisons are exploratory and were not multiplicity adjusted.
The largest positive and negative class changes generally occurred in classes
with single-digit test support.

Labeled-host oracle masking was applied to fixed condition logits without
retraining. It increased mean accuracy by 2.294 points for the flat model and
2.184 points for the dual model; macro-F1 increased by 10.601 and 10.232 points,
respectively. The oracle is non-deployable and sets CHER to zero by construction.
It estimates the error removable by perfect host information rather than the
performance of a practical host sensor.

For every primary flat and dual checkpoint, one temperature was fitted only on
validation logits and then applied to locked-test logits. Temperature scaling
reduced ECE, NLL, and summed multiclass Brier score in all six
 dataset and configuration cells. AURC changed little, as expected for a monotonic
single-temperature transformation that largely preserves confidence ranking.

  \item seed-level primary metrics and paired differences, TOST/Holm outputs,

  \item family-balanced, penalty, HDR/TCI, disagreement-decomposition,

  outputs;
  \item the executed Agri-Foundation-145k placeholder-class sensitivity
  results, including the audit record for the $10\times10$ file;
  \item scripts used to train models, validate runs, regenerate tables and
  figures, and recompute checksums; and
  \item a concise README that maps every manuscript table and figure to its
  generating files and command.
\end{itemize}

contain every machine-readable result file named in earlier draft notes.

the supplied text, tables, figures, configuration files, and scripts; it does
